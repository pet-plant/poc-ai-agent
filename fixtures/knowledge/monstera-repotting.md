# Monstera Deliciosa: Repotting Instructions

**Species:** Monstera deliciosa  
**Topic:** repotting  

Monstera deliciosa plants typically require repotting every two to three years as roots outgrow their container.

## Repotting Steps
1. Select a pot that is 2 inches wider in diameter with ample drainage holes.
2. Prepare a chunky, airy soil mix containing peat, perlite, and orchid bark.
3. Gently loosen bound roots and center the plant in the new container.
4. Support climbing stems with a sturdy moss pole or wooden trellis.
