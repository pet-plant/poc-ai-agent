# Monstera Deliciosa: Watering Guidelines

**Species:** Monstera deliciosa  
**Topic:** watering  

Monstera deliciosa thrives in moist but well-draining soil. It is very sensitive to sitting in soggy, waterlogged soil, which quickly leads to root suffocation and root rot.

## Watering Instructions
1. Allow the top 2 to 3 inches of soil to completely dry out before watering again.
2. When watering, saturate thoroughly until water drains from the bottom drainage holes, then empty the drip tray.
3. Reduce watering frequency significantly during cooler autumn and winter months.
4. If the plant shows drooping leaves with dry soil, water immediately.
