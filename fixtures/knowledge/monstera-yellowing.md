# Monstera Deliciosa: Causes and Treatment of Yellowing Leaves

**Species:** Monstera deliciosa  
**Topic:** leaf_yellowing  

Yellow leaves (chlorosis) on a Monstera deliciosa are the most common sign of watering or drainage distress.

## Primary Causes & Care Actions
1. **Overwatering & Poor Drainage (Most Common):** The lower leaves turn pale yellow and limp. Soil remains wet or soggy for days.
   - **Action:** Stop watering immediately. Check soil moisture 2 inches down. Allow soil to dry out thoroughly. Ensure drainage holes are unblocked.
2. **Underwatering:** Leaves become yellow, brittle, and develop dry brown crisp edges.
   - **Action:** Deeply soak the root ball and resume consistent watering when top 2 inches dry.
3. **Nutrient Deficiency / Low Light:** General pale yellowing across older foliage.
   - **Action:** Move to bright, indirect sunlight and apply balanced liquid fertilizer.
